Where quantum computing may matter

Potential applications

• Simulating molecules and materials
• Research into some optimization methods
• Studying quantum systems
• Certain cryptography-related tasks

Limits and realities

• Qubits are fragile and noisy
• Error correction is difficult
• Most tasks still suit classical computers
• Practical advantage must be demonstrated for each task
Important: Quantum computers do not try every possible answer and automatically select the correct one. Algorithms use quantum effects to shape measurement outcomes for particular problems.
