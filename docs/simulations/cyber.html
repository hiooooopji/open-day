Core security principles

The CIA triad is a foundation of information security.
Confidentiality

Keep information private and accessible only to authorized people. Use access controls and encryption.

Integrity

Keep information accurate and protected from unauthorized changes. Use validation and trusted records.

Availability

Keep systems and data accessible when needed. Use backups, maintenance, and recovery plans.

Cybersecurity combines technology and human decisions. No single tool blocks every threat, so layered protection matters.
