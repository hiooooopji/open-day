What to notice

A generative model does not simply retrieve one fixed answer. It uses patterns learned from training data to produce new content. Results can be useful, incorrect, biased, or incomplete, so human review still matters.
Important distinction: Generative AI focuses on creating content. Agentic AI focuses on pursuing a goal through steps and tool use. An agent can use a generative model as its reasoning or content-generation engine.
